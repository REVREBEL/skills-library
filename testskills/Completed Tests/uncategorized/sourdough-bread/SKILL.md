# Sourdough Bread

## Steps
1. Feed starter with equal weights flour and water.
2. Wait 8-12 hours for active bubbles.
3. Mix flour, water, salt, and starter.
4. Fold dough every 30 minutes for 2 hours.
5. Bulk ferment 4-6 hours.
6. Shape and cold-proof overnight.
7. Bake at 250C in a Dutch oven.
